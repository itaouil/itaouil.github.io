I am a PhD student at the <a href='https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/aipd/home/'>ATARI Lab</a> and the <a href='https://www.3dunderstanding.org/'>3D AI Lab</a> at the Technical University of Munich.

My research sits at the intersection of robotics, 3D computer vision, and learning. My primary focus is developing frameworks that allow humanoid robots to plan in unseen environments in order to accomplish long-horizon goals.

P.S. I am constantly looking for exceptional master's students interested in contributing to our research on humanoind robots. If you are enthusiastic about developing advanced algorithms and applying them to real-world legged robots, please get in touch!
